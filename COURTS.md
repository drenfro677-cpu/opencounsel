# What all United States court files means

There is no single ZIP of every American filing.

## Directory (in the app)
CourtListener courts API: 3,359 courts.
F 127 federal appellate / related · FD 125 district · FB 95 bankruptcy · S 55 state high · SA 111 state appellate · ST 2618 state trial · SS 86 specialty.
GET https://www.courtlistener.com/api/rest/v4/courts/

## Opinions

## Dockets / PDFs of filings
Federal: PACER (paid) + RECAP (only what someone fetched).
State trial: county CMS. No national free dump.
Do not scrape.
